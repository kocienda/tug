- *The PULSE summaries*: I have not been satisfied with them thus far, so locking in the current behavior seems foolish. Again, the PULSE is design to be a two-part information display, where the model delivers the first-part/intent by summarizing the transcript as it flows by. These *intents* should be biased substantially by the latest prompt/message/declaration-of-intent from the user, and yet they should still continue to change as the assistant/shell/machinery whirs and churns to satify that intent. The current six-word limitation is too strict. Let's go instead for 64ch. Finally, once a session goes *idle* after doing work, the intent should collapse into a summary of *what was actually done*, and not just strand the PULSE with the last intent it produced.
