# Typed Host Messages

**Purpose:** The host vocabulary ([L39]) declares every message that crosses between a page and the Swift host, but 22 page→host names, 16 host→page members and 4 page calls still carry their payloads loose, and their handlers read `[String: Any]` field by field with defaults — the very reading the law says never happens. Type or retire every one of them, so the law's text and the code say the same thing.

---

## Purpose {#purpose}

The host-channel-and-body-deck arc declared the whole channel and typed the geometry family: everything the one-view card reads or writes decodes into a `Decodable` struct or is refused whole. Its brief's [B02] let the rest ride loose "until the change that next touches it types it." The audit of that arc found the carve-out had become the majority of the vocabulary:

> Twenty-two of the 36 page-to-host names and 16 of the 21 host-to-page members are still carried loose, and their handlers in `MainWindow.swift` still read fields with defaults.

The user's answer to the audit's question — fix the nits on `main`, and the loose messages? — was to write this brief. Typing them is a multi-sitting change to the live host channel with a wide derived app-test selection, which is the arc machinery's shape and not a `main` fixup's.

What is wrong is not that the loose messages misbehave today. It is that [L39] already reads "a payload that does not decode is refused whole and logged by name, never read field by field with a default", and `MainWindow.receive` does exactly that for most of its cases. A law the code contradicts teaches every reader that the laws are aspirational.

---

## Evidence {#evidence}

**[F01] The loose census.** `tugdeck/src/host/declarations/services.ts` and `lifecycle.ts` declare, with `looseObject`, `looseString` or `looseNumber` codecs and `typed: false`: page→host `sourceTree`, `choosePath`, `getSettings`, `setTheme`, `devBadge`, `clipboardRead`, `clipboardWrite`, `clipboardWriteImage`, `menuState`, `openPath`, `lookUpInDictionary`, `trashPath`, `restorePath`, `thumbnailPath`, `exportSession`, `updateAction` (a bare string), `dictation`, `pageZoom`, `hostFrames`, `frontendLaunchStalled`, `pageFault`, `hmrUpdate` (22); host→page `onSettingsLoaded`, `onDevModeError`, `onSourceTreeSelected`, `onSourceTreeCancelled`, `onPathChosen`, `onExportDone`, `onClipboardRead`, `onTrashResult`, `onThumbnail`, `onHostFrames`, `onDictation`, `onUpdateState`, `onPageZoom`, `onActivationClick`, `onNetworkPath`, `onScrollPhase` (16); and every page call — `saveState`, `prepareForTermination`, `reauthenticate` (`looseObject`/`looseReply`) and `onPageZoomApply` (`looseNumber`/`looseReply`) (4). Every one already carries `samples` and a `refused` list, but the parity tests check refusals only where `typed` is true (`host-parity.test.ts`, `HostChannelTests.swift:101,171`), so a loose entry's refusals are decoration. **(verified, by reading the declarations and the two parity tests)**

**[F02] The Swift twin carries the loose half as `HostObject` and `JSONValue`.** `tugapp/Sources/HostMessages.swift`: `struct HostObject { let fields: [String: Any] }`, 21 `ToHost` cases wrapping it, a `loose(_:_:)` switch that exists only to wrap, a `decode` that special-cases `updateAction` as the one bare string, `ToPageMember.carriesString` and `.typed` switches, `ToPage.loose`/`.text`/`.make`, and `HostChannel.send(_ member:, _ value: Any, …)` which builds a `JSONValue` from a Foundation dictionary at send time. All of it exists to carry a payload nobody decoded. **(verified)**

**[F03] The handlers read with defaults.** `MainWindow.receive(_:from:)` has 43 `as?` casts across its loose cases. Among them: `(body["kind"] as? String) ?? "directory"` (`choosePath`), `(body["baseName"] as? String) ?? "tug-session"` and two `?? ""` (`exportSession`), `body["backend"] as? Bool ?? false` (`devBadge`), `(body["windowMs"] as? NSNumber)?.intValue ?? 2000` (`hostFrames`), `body["waitingOn"] as? String` falling to `"unknown"` (`frontendLaunchStalled`), and `guard let … else { return }` on `requestId`/`id`/`color`/`factor` — a silent drop of a malformed message with no log, where the typed path logs a refusal by name. `PageFaultScript.log(_ body: Any?)` reads `kind`/`message`/`source` out of `as? [String: Any]` with defaults. **(verified, by reading `MainWindow.swift:2245–2720` and `PageFaultScript.swift:78–93`)**

**[F04] `menuState` is the one deep payload, and it already has a type on the page.** `lib/host-menu-state.ts` declares `MenuStatePayload` with `panes[]`, `spaces[]`, `activeCard`, `selectionActive`, `stackDepth`, `session`, `file`, `document`, `edit` and `commands` (a map of gates each carrying `chords`, where a chord is a `ChordSpec | null`). The declaration's codec is `looseObject({})` and its sample is three fields of a shape that does not match the interface. On the host, `MenuState.swift` parses it with 14 `[String: Any]` casts in `init(payload:)` and `merged(shell:bodies:)` merges the shell's and the key body's dictionaries by key; `AppDelegate.swift` keeps the dictionaries. A comment at `MenuState.swift:257` records that `as? [String: Any]` alone collapses a chord's `null` and its absence, which a `Decodable` with an optional of an optional distinguishes for free. **(verified)**

**[F05] Host→page payloads are built from typed Swift values and flattened by hand.** `UpdateStateSnapshot.jsonObject: [String: Any]` (`UpdateState.swift:203`), `DictationEvent.jsonObject` (`DictationState.swift:131`, whose doc says its optional fields are "the union of every kind's payload"), `HostFrameMeter.read() -> [String: Any]` (`HostFrameMeter.swift:328`, "in the harness's shape"), and the `replyToTrashRequest`/`replyToThumbnailRequest`/`replyToBridgeRequest` helpers in `MainWindow.swift:1651–1668` all take or make dictionaries that `ToPage.make` re-reads into `JSONValue`. The sender already knows the type; the dictionary is the only untyped link. **(verified)**

**[F06] Four members are declared dead by their own doc comments.** `devBadge` — "Swift answers it; no page writes it today": no `postToHost(devBadgeToHost…)` exists, and the chain `MainWindow` → `bridgeDevBadge` → `BridgeHandler.swift:238` has no caller but the dead case. `onDevModeError`, `onSourceTreeSelected`, `onSourceTreeCancelled` — "No page receives it today": no `onHostMessage` registers any of them, yet `MainWindow.swift:1866` and `:2254–2256` still send them. `sourceTree` itself is live (`action-dispatch.ts:554`), so the picker opens; its two replies fall on no listener. **(verified, by grepping the page for each declaration's symbol)**

**[F07] The guard is scoped to four files.** `host/__tests__/no-hand-channel.test.ts` bans `[String: Any]` only in `BodyViewController.swift`, `BodyGeometry.swift`, `BodyBeats.swift` and `CardGeometry.swift`. Outside `TestHarness/`, the type appears in `MenuState.swift` (14), `ProcessManager.swift` (7), `HostFrameMeter.swift` (5), `AppDelegate.swift` (5), `MainWindow.swift` (4), `HostMessages.swift` (3), `ControlSocket.swift` (3), `DictationState.swift` (2), and one each in `UpdateState.swift`, `PageFaultScript.swift` and `FrameMeterStats.swift`. `ProcessManager` and `ControlSocket` read tugcast's and the control socket's JSON, which are not host messages. **(verified, by count)**

**[F08] The law and the recipe already assume the typed shape.** [L39] in `tuglaws/tuglaws.md` says refused whole, never read with a default, with no loose exception; `shell-and-bodies.md#adding-a-host-message` step 1 asks for "the `refused` payloads a typed codec must refuse" and step 3 for "a `Decodable` payload a typed message decodes into", with loose as the unstated other case. `services.ts`'s header cites the prior brief's [B02] as its licence. **(verified)**

## Decisions {#decisions}

**[B01] Every host message is typed, and the loose kind is deleted.** The page side: `looseObject`, `looseString`, `looseNumber` and `looseReply` go from `host/declare.ts`, along with the `typed` flag on `ToHost`/`ToPage` declarations — a flag that is always true says nothing. Each declaration gets a real codec from `bus/codec` (`obj`, `str`, `num`, `bool`, `nullable`, `optional`, `literal`, `opaque`), as the geometry family's do. The Swift side: `HostObject`, the `loose(_:_:)` switch, the `updateAction` special case in `decode`, `ToPageMember.carriesString`/`.typed`, `ToPage.loose`/`.text`/`.make` and `HostChannel.send(_ member:, _ value: Any, …)` go; every `ToHost` case carries a `Decodable` struct (or nothing, for an `{}` payload, via `EmptyPayload`) and every `ToPage` case an `Encodable` one. A string payload (`updateAction`, `onScrollPhase`) is a `String`-backed enum of its literals, not a free string. Why: the prior brief's carve-out was a scheduling choice, not a design — it let the geometry arc land without typing the clipboard. With the geometry typed and the machinery proven on sixteen messages, the remaining cost is census work, and the price of leaving it is a law the code contradicts [F08]. `JSONValue` stays only if a payload genuinely carries arbitrary JSON (a body's `intent` does, via `JSONObject`); it is no longer the carrier of anything else.

*Resolved in the building:* a page call's reply is typed as well as its payload. The page declares each call's `replies` and `refusedReplies` beside its samples; three calls answer `null` (`noReply`) and `prepareForTermination` answers its `TerminationVerdict`, which the host decodes as `PageReply.verdict` and logs field by field. A page with no such member answers nothing (`PageReply.nothing`), and a reply that does not decode is logged and fails, so the quit pipeline names it unreadable and saves synchronously. `JSONValue` keeps one more job beyond `JSONObject`: WebKit hands a call's reply over as Foundation values, and `JSONValue(any:)` is the bridge from those to the bytes `PageReply` decodes. The fixture's page-call rows carry `refused`, `replies` and `refusedReplies`, and `HostChannelTests` checks that every call the host makes sends one of the page's samples.

**[B02] The parity fixture drops `typed` and checks every entry's refusals on both sides.** With every message typed, the fixture's `typed` column goes and `host-parity.test.ts`/`HostChannelTests.swift` run the refusal loop over every entry [F01]. The `refused` lists already written on every loose declaration are the first refusals the new codecs must make, kept as they are and extended where a codec's shape invites a wrong-typed field (`{ factor: "1.25" }`, `{ requestId: 7 }`).

**[B03] A field the writer always writes is required; one it may omit is optional, and the handler names its default once.** This is the geometry family's rule, restated for the services. The census is done from the page's `postToHost` call sites and the host's senders, not from the handler's current reads: `choosePath`'s `kind` is always written by `native-path-picker.ts`, so `?? "directory"` goes; `exportSession` always carries `baseName`, `markdown` and `jsonl`, so the three defaults go. Where the handler's default was covering for a writer that really can omit the field, the declaration says `optional(...)` and the default stays — written beside the decode, as `BodyViewController` does, not deep in the case. A `guard let … else { return }` on a required field becomes a decode failure the channel logs by name, which is what the law asks and what the silent drop [F03] denied.

**[B04] A payload that varies by kind is a discriminated union on both sides, not a union of every kind's optional fields.** `onDictation` (`ready`, `volatile`, `ended`…), `onTrashResult` (`ok: true` with `trashedPath` or `ok: false` with `error`), `onUpdateState` (stages with stage-specific fields), `onThumbnail` (`dataUrl` or `null`) and `dictation`'s verbs are each declared as a tagged union on the page and a Swift enum with associated values whose `Codable` is keyed on the tag. `DictationEvent.jsonObject`'s "union of every kind's payload" [F05] is the shape this rules out: it lets a `volatile` event omit its text and a `ready` event carry one. Where a union's variants share most of their fields (`onUpdateState`'s thirteen), the arc may keep one struct with optionals if it writes down why; the default is the union.

*Resolved in the building:* `onUpdateState` keeps one object, because the host writes all thirteen fields in every stage (an `idle` snapshot still carries the running version and the reveal count) and the store keeps the snapshot whole, so a stage's variant would differ only in which fields the deck reads; the reason is written on `updateStateToPage` and on `UpdateSnapshot`'s `Codable`. `onDictation` is the union on both sides, the reserved `level` kind included, so the two vocabularies stay one; `updateAction` and `onScrollPhase` are literal sets. Typing `dictation` surfaced that a body page minted the same `dict-1` as the shell and that a superseded claim's `ended` went to the page that took the mic, and that body pages never heard the app resign; the ids now carry a per-page mark, the host routes each event to the page whose claim it is, and the host sends `app-lifecycle` to every page.

**[B05] Host→page payloads are encoded from the Swift value the sender already holds.** `UpdateStateSnapshot`, `DictationEvent`, the frame meter's reading, the path/export/trash/thumbnail replies and the settings reply become `Encodable` types carried by their `ToPage` case; the `jsonObject` properties and the `replyTo…Request(_ payload: [String: Any])` helpers go. `HostFrameMeter.read()` serves the harness too ("in the harness's shape"), so its reading becomes one `Encodable` struct that both the host message and the harness's JSON encode from — one shape, not two.

**[B06] `menuState` is a struct on the host, and the merge is over the struct.** `MenuStatePayload` already exists on the page [F04]; the declaration's codec encodes that interface, and `MenuState.swift` gains the `Decodable` twin, with `init(payload:)` becoming a plain decode and `merged(shell:bodies:)` taking and returning the struct. `AppDelegate` keeps the structs. The null-versus-absent chord distinction the comment at `MenuState.swift:257` worked around by hand is carried by `optional(nullable(chordSpec))` on the page and `ChordSpec??` on the host. This is the one message whose typing changes a shape the menus are built from, so its verdict run includes the menu-state app-tests `@covers` resolves for `host-menu-state.ts` and `MenuState.swift`.

*Resolved in the building:* a body page posts only its card's blocks (`session`, `file`, `document`, `edit`, `commands`), which the page had cast to the whole payload, so the declaration is a union of the shell's whole `MenuStatePayload` and a body's `MenuStateBodyPayload`, told apart by `panes`, which only the whole carries, so a malformed whole is refused rather than read as its blocks; the host's twin is `MenuStatePost`, `.shell(MenuState)` or `.body(MenuState.CardBlocks)`, and `merged(shell:bodies:)` takes the shell's state and the bodies' blocks. The host decodes only the fields it reads. `MenuState`'s "a malformed payload reads as its inert value" is reversed by [B01]: a malformed push is refused whole and the menus keep the last state that decoded. With no loose page→host message left, the to-host loose path and `HostObject` went here rather than with the page calls. The verdict run surfaced that View ▸ Zoom on a PDF had stopped reaching the card when cards moved into body pages: the host's `zoom-*` tell is now addressed to the claiming card's page, and the zoom ladder steps past a stop within 2% of the current scale, since fit-width can land a hair below one.

**[B07] The four dead members are deleted, with their sends and delegate chain.** `devBadge` and `bridgeDevBadge` down to `BridgeHandler.swift:238`; `onDevModeError` and the `MainWindow.swift:1866` send (the `ProcessManager.onDevModeError` log line stays — the host still knows, the page never asked); `onSourceTreeSelected` and `onSourceTreeCancelled` and their sends at `:2254–2256`, leaving `sourceTree`'s handler to open the picker and let the delegate persist the choice. One condition: before deleting the two source-tree replies, the arc reads `bridgeChooseSourceTree`'s delegate (`BridgeHandler.swift:23`) and confirms the page learns the chosen tree another way (it re-asks via `getSettings`/`onSettingsLoaded`, or the host relaunches). If nothing re-reads it, the member is not dead but unreceived — a latent bug — and it is revived typed with a page-side receiver instead of deleted. The user asked for the dead members to go in the audit's turn; this decision records the removal so the arc does not re-ask.

**[B08] The `[String: Any]` guard covers every host-message file, by naming the files that are not.** `no-hand-channel.test.ts`'s four-file geometry list becomes: every `.swift` under `tugapp/Sources/` outside `TestHarness/`, minus a named allow-list of files whose JSON is not a host message's — `ProcessManager.swift` and `ControlSocket.swift` [F07], and whatever else the census shows reads tugcast, Sparkle or the control socket rather than a page. `HostMessages.swift` is not on the allow-list: once [B01] lands it has no reason to name the type. The test's name for the assertion changes from "no geometry file" to "no host-message file", and the allow-list's comment says what each file's JSON is instead.

**[B09] `services.ts`'s header, the recipe and the law lose the loose clause.** The header's "Every payload here is loose ([B02]) until the change that next touches it types it" goes. `shell-and-bodies.md#adding-a-host-message` steps 1 and 3 drop "typed" as a qualifier, since there is no other kind. [L39]'s text already reads as typed [F08] and needs no change; the arc checks that the law's example list still names real messages. A design decision is recorded for the deletion of the loose kind, as [D215] recorded the bus's typed-vocabulary retirement, so the carve-out is not re-proposed as a convenience.

## Non-goals {#non-goals}

- **Changing what any message means or when it is sent.** This types the payloads that cross today; it does not redesign the clipboard, dictation or update flows, move a message onto the bus, or change which page sends or receives one. A message whose census turns up a field nobody reads loses the field; a message whose census turns up a reader for a field nobody writes is a bug reported in the arc's commit, not fixed here.
- **Typing `JSONObject` carriers.** A body's `intent` is arbitrary JSON by design and stays `JSONObject`/`opaque`.
- **A `[String: Any]` ban on files that parse tugcast's, Sparkle's or the control socket's JSON.** Those are other channels with other laws; [B08] names them out.
- **Keeping a loose kind "for the next new message".** Considered and rejected: the recipe makes a typed declaration four lines longer than a loose one, and the carve-out is how the vocabulary got to 42 loose entries. A new message is typed or it is not declared.
- **Reviving `onDevModeError`, `onSourceTreeSelected` or `onSourceTreeCancelled` with page receivers** — except under [B07]'s condition. A page that wants dev-mode errors surfaced is a feature, and it should be asked for.
- **The two row-17 app-test reds** (`row-17-crossing-bullseye-in`, `row-17-crossing-kinds-resize`) found during the audit. They are the shell-beneath arc's placement model, not the channel's typing, and belong to their own brief.

## Exit {#exit}

**An arc.** The shape its first steps take, in the order the dependencies force:

1. **Delete the dead members** [B07], after the `bridgeChooseSourceTree` check. Four declarations, four `ToHostName`/`ToPageMember` cases, three sends, one delegate chain; regenerate the parity fixture. Smallest first, and it removes two of the three string-carrying members before [B01] has to decide what a string member is.
2. **Type the simple objects**, one group per step, each regenerating the fixture and running its `@covers` selection: the request/reply pairs (`choosePath`/`onPathChosen`, `clipboardRead`/`onClipboardRead`, `trashPath`/`restorePath`/`onTrashResult`, `thumbnailPath`/`onThumbnail`, `exportSession`/`onExportDone`, `getSettings`/`onSettingsLoaded`, `hostFrames`/`onHostFrames`), then the one-way posts (`setTheme`, `pageZoom`/`onPageZoom`/`onPageZoomApply`, `openPath`, `lookUpInDictionary`, `clipboardWrite`, `clipboardWriteImage`, `onActivationClick`, `onNetworkPath`, `frontendLaunchStalled`, `pageFault`, `hmrUpdate`, `sourceTree`), with [B05]'s `Encodable` senders landing beside each reply.
3. **Type the unions** [B04]: `dictation`/`onDictation`, `onUpdateState`/`updateAction`, `onScrollPhase`.
4. **Type `menuState`** [B06], with the menu app-tests as its verdict.
5. **Type the page calls** (`saveState`, `prepareForTermination`, `reauthenticate`, `onPageZoomApply`'s reply) and **delete the loose kind** [B01]: `declare.ts`'s four helpers and the `typed` flag, `HostObject`, `ToPage.loose`/`.text`/`.make`, `send(_ member:, _ value: Any…)`, the `carriesString`/`typed` switches; the fixture drops its `typed` column [B02].
6. **Widen the guard** [B08] and **retire the clause** [B09]: the `[String: Any]` allow-list, the header, the recipe, the design decision.

Steps 2 to 4 are independent of one another and may land in any order; 5 depends on all of them, since the loose kind cannot go while anything uses it; 6 depends on 5. Each step's verdict is its own `@covers` selection, and the Swift side's is `TugTests` plus `just app-test-build`.
