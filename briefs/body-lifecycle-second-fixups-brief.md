# The body lifecycle's second fixups: data loss, hangs, dead code and the state machines behind them

**Purpose:** The body lifecycle fixups arc (`0b5a77e30`) closed most of what the first audit found, and four arcs then landed on the same code: the Layout card rows were removed (`4f3c12e34`), the staged-bodies lane and kept-on-reload bodies (`06e4041de`), the switch hand-over's host faces (`3dc927ffe`), and the host-carried rail drag (`1e62251d5`, `34d4ac0aa`). A second 360° audit on `main` at `cd6804c69` found three defects that lose work or freeze a card, the cover leak the first round meant to close, a layer of code nothing runs, decisions the documents no longer tell the truth about, and two structural causes behind nearly every defect. This brief carries every fix, most important first.

---

## Purpose {#purpose}

The user: "I want one more 360° audit on this work. Take all the latest changes into account. Then, answer... Do we need to make fixups? Or, can we move on from here with confidence that the codebase is in good shape?"

The answer was fixups first. The architecture holds — the session lives apart from the page, the host owns the clock and the pressure response, the shell owns intent and stand-ins, every reveal is a host-side transaction, nothing caps the number of cards — and the later arcs landed on it without fighting it. What does not hold is a handful of paths where one role forgets another role's flag, and a set of documents and code left behind when the Layout card rows went.

The standing rules this brief answers to: no dead code and no behaviours that exist for tests; a decision reversed is said in the open in the document that records it; a failing test has three endings and "known red" is not one; a real fault with a fix gets fixed.

### Defects that lose work or hang a card

**[F01] An evicted Text card closes without its save prompt.** The `evicted` handler (`tugdeck/src/lib/body-shell.ts:1602`) calls `bus.detach`. `card.holds` is `clearOnDetach` (`bus/declarations/card.ts:214`), so after the detach `cardHolds.getFor(cardId).closeGuardAsks` reads `null`, and the shell's guard (`body-shell.ts:796`, `needsDecision`) says no decision is needed. Staged bodies make `guardsClose` kinds at once for exactly this reason (`lib/body-handoff.ts:245`: "only its page can answer that guard"), but `evictable` (`tugapp/Sources/BodyViewController.swift:2652`) never reads the kind. A Text card with unsaved edits, hidden fifteen minutes in an inactive tab, is evicted; closing the tab discards the edits with no prompt. **(verified, read end to end)**

**[F02] An abandoned catch-up bracket hangs the card for good.** `replay_started` cancels the `catch_up` timer (`tugdeck/src/lib/code-session-store/reducer.ts:8494`). If that bracket is then abandoned — `tick_replay_silence` at 15 s, or `tick_replay_bracket` at 150 s — `abandonBracket` (`reducer.ts:7648`) enters `errored` but clears neither `replayCatchUpActive` nor `replayReask`. `catchUpAwaitsBracket` (`reducer.ts:1239`) then returns true for every phase but `replaying`, and the gate at `reducer.ts:8313` drops every later turn frame and every `replay_complete`. On the page, `catchUpCard` (`body/body-main.ts:346`) waits on `catchingUp`, so the lid stays up and the body never wakes. A prompt the user sends gets no visible reply. Reproduced with a reducer probe: `begin_catch_up → replay_started → tick_replay_silence` leaves `phase=errored, catchUp=true`, after which `add_user_message`, `assistant_text` and `replay_complete` are all dropped. **(verified, probed)**

**[F03] A card that went dormant under a shadow loses its whole catch-up.** `replay_started` is in `SHADOWED_FRAME_TYPES` (`reducer.ts:1212`). A body that sleeps while `replayShadowActive` never hears that shadow's `replay_complete`, so the shadow stands until `REPLAY_SHADOW_DEADLINE_MS` (150 s). Revealed inside that window, `catchUpAwaitsBracket` is false (the shadow is active), the catch-up's `replay_started` is dropped at `reducer.ts:8321` before `supersedesCycle` is reached, and the bracket's turns go with it; its `replay_complete` lifts the shadow and ends the catch-up as if it had succeeded. Probe: an idle card with an `inflight_only` shadow, then `begin_catch_up → replay_started → replay_complete`, ends `phase=idle`, catch-up over, nothing entered. Dormancy starts at 60 s against the 150 s horizon, so this is reachable; it is the first audit's F08 loss by another door. **(verified, probed)**

**[F04] Covers still pin their images for `evictAfterMs`.** The aging closure in `keep()` (`BodyViewController.swift:2867`) captures the whole `BodyCover`, `CGImage` included, for the fifteen-minute timer. So the 32 MB budget trim (`:2862`), `covers.removeAll()` under critical (`:2638`), `destroy` (`:2491`) and `standCover` (`:857`) free nothing until the timer fires. A warning episode that evicts thirty window-sized cards trims the map to 32 MB and keeps about 150 MB resident for fifteen minutes; critical frees none of it. The first audit's F04 is not closed. **(verified, read)**

### Real but bounded

**[F05] `destroy(save: true)` has no deadline.** At `BodyViewController.swift:2503` `remove` waits on a `callAsyncJavaScript` with no timeout (`HostChannel.swift:160`). A page that hangs stays in `bodies` for ever: its process leaks, the tombstone (`gone`) makes it unevictable (`:2653`), `body(forCard:)` still returns it so a later `create` for the card is dropped as "has a body; ignoring" (`:741`), a body still loading holds a lane slot for ever (`inFlight`, `:788`; two such bodies stall the lane), and `destroy` does not cancel `body.deadline`, so a closed card that was not ready gets `failed` reported (`:2900`). `reloadForEdit` already races its save against `saveDeadline`. **(verified, read)**

**[F06] A hidden body whose process dies is reloaded at once, outside the lane.** `webContentProcessDidTerminate` → `loadAfresh` (`:2963`, `:2994`) checks no visibility and does not queue in the lane. Under memory pressure, which is when WebKit and jetsam kill background processes first, every hidden or dormant body comes back in parallel while memory is shortest. `loadAfresh` sets `shownOnce = false`, so the reloaded hidden page stands unhidden at opacity 0 (`:1062`) and is never throttled again until shown; `shown.since` is not reset, so a body with earlier declines gets `armEviction` with a delay of 0 at `didBecomeReady` (`:987`) and is evicted straight after a full reload. **(path verified; the trigger is inference)**

**[F07] The catch-up re-ask is dropped by tugcode when the old bracket still runs, and then hangs.** `replay-runner.ts:416` drops any `request_replay` that arrives while a replay is in flight (`replay_in_flight`). The old bracket's tail, its `replay_complete` included, is then dropped by the catch-up gate (phase `replaying`, re-ask not opened), `tick_catch_up` does nothing inside `replaying` (`reducer.ts:8547`), and the silence deadline carries the card into [F02]'s hang. **(verified in code; the timing is inference)**

**[F08] The re-ask path is mostly unreachable, and the user sees a false stall.** A bracket's silence timer keeps running while the page sleeps (`replaySilenceEffect`, `reducer.ts:1272`). **[F13] Smaller host faults.** A warning-era eviction whose `takeCover` is in flight when critical arrives finishes afterwards, and `keep()` (`:2769`) puts a cover back into the map critical just emptied. `reloadForEdit` or a termination on a mid-eviction body leaves `evicting` true, snapshots a loading page, and the shell hears `terminated` then `evicted`; a termination of a `gone` body reloads a closed card's page. `declined` holds `ObjectIdentifier`s (`:542`), which can be reused once a body is freed. ## Decisions {#decisions}

Most important first; a plan walks them in this order.

**[B01] An evicted card never loses a guard only its page can answer.** A kind that `guardsClose` is exempt from eviction by time and by warning (`evictable` reads the kind the body was made for), and under critical pressure the shell keeps the last `closeGuardAsks` reading the page published rather than clearing it with the detach, so a close after any eviction still asks. Both halves, because critical must still be able to take every hidden page. Closes [F01]; a row evicts a dirty Text card and closes it, and the prompt stands.

**[B02] The catch-up has exactly one exit on every path, and it is a state, not a flag.** `replayCatchUpActive`, `replayReask`, `replayAskedWindow` and `replayOpenWindow` become one `catchUp` sub-state with a declared set of legal transitions: asked → open → complete; asked → deadline; open → abandoned. `abandonBracket` ends it. A re-ask the runner drops (`replay_in_flight`) is detected by the old bracket's `replay_complete` arriving in `asked`, which is answered by asking again, not by dropping it. The silence and bracket timers are suspended while the page sleeps and resumed from the reveal, so a sleeping card never wakes to a false stall. `replay_started` leaves `SHADOWED_FRAME_TYPES` when a catch-up is asked, and a shadow standing at sleep is dropped at the reveal so the catch-up's bracket is the authority (the catch-up supersedes the shadow by construction, since it was asked after everything the shadow stood for). Frames set aside before the bracket are kept and re-applied if the deadline passes with no bracket, never dropped. Closes [F02], [F03], [F07], [F08], [F09]; a reducer test drives each exit, and reducer tests cover a shadow at sleep, a dropped re-ask, the silence timer during sleep, a late bracket, and a user send during the catch-up ([F15] is fixed or shown not to arise by that test).

**[B03] `heldTurnsOf` names only turns.** Ink and refs entries, and any entry whose `msgId` is a minted `runId`, are neither counted in `heldTurns` nor named among the sixteen ids. The stub's `turnAfter` is replaced by the production `turnAfterHeld`, so the two cannot drift. Closes [F10] and the stub leftover in [F21].

**[B04] Covers hold nothing past the map.** The aging timer captures `takenAt` alone, never the cover; dropping a cover from the map is the last reference to its image. Critical-pressure clearing also cancels in-flight `takeCover`s, or `keep()` refuses a cover arriving after the clear. A body that has not painted is never snapshotted: its standing cover, if any, is kept. Closes [F04] and the two cover items of [F13]; a Swift unit test asserts the image is released when the map drops it.

**[B05] One body state, one authority.** `Body`'s `evicting`, `gone`, `keptAcrossShellReload`, `shownOnce`, `painted` and `cover` flags become one `BodyState` enum (made, loading, ready, evicting, evicted-with-cover, gone, with `seen`/`painted` as facts the enum carries), and every transition goes through one function that rejects an illegal one. `destroy` races its save against `saveDeadline` and cancels `body.deadline` ([F05]). A termination of an unseen body is an eviction with no cover: remove and report `evicted`, never `loadAfresh` ([F06]); a termination of a `gone` body does nothing. `reloadForEdit` and termination on an `evicting` body abandon the eviction first. `declined` is keyed by body serial, not `ObjectIdentifier`. A warning episode re-asks a declined body at most once. A reloaded shell is told the current pressure level on its hello. Closes [F05], [F06], the rest of [F13]. The enum is the fix for this class of defect, not a refactor beside it.
