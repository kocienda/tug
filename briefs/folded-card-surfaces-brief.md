# The folded card's one surface: sheets, inline dialogs and Z2 placards on a folded Session card

**Purpose:** A folded Session card shows a masthead and its Z2 status row and nothing else. Everything the card raises while folded — a sheet, a compaction cover, a question, a permission request, a Z2 placard — is either painted into the space the fold took away, or hung off a 144px frame over the neighbour below. None of it reads as the folded card's own. The card needs one rule for what appears where when it is folded, and the surfaces need to follow it.

---

## Purpose {#purpose}

In the user's words, on seeing the `sheet-visibility` arc's folded-card result (a full-height AI settings panel dropping from a folded masthead over the card beneath):

> This *can't be* the compacted *show the sheet when folded* experience. Needs a complete rethink. I think this should take the form of a sheet presentation that *takes over the screen space given to Z2*. When we are folded, then compact, inline dialogs, and other sheets should be shown in that space. For presentations like *Show Usage* and *AI...* (which should be renamed in the Swift menu to *AI Settings...*), we should show an alert-like presentation in Z2 to lead the user to unfold/expand the card to see this content. Compact sheet should fit. I think the rest should show a prompt to actually deal with or display the inline dialog or sheet content.

And, on the Z2 telemetry placards:

> The Z2 popups *are not displaying* and they must. They can and should overhang, I think, and just point to their item.

The first complaint is about what the last arc built. The second is a defect that arc's brief explicitly waved past (its [F08] said the placards were canvas-tier and unaffected, "not reproduced on a folded card"). Both come from the same missing rule: a folded card was treated as a very short card, and it is not one. It is a card that has chosen to show one row.

## Evidence {#evidence}

**[F01] At rest a folded Session card is its masthead and its Z2 row, and nothing else has a box.** `session-card.css:1584` takes the view slot out of layout (`.session-card[data-fold="settled"] .session-view-slot { display: none }`); the entry region is a `grid-template-rows: 0fr` track under `.tug-pane[data-folded="true"]` (`:1542`) and is `inert` along with the slot (the fold effect in `session-card.tsx` around `:3545`). The top column becomes content-sized at rest (`:1577`) "so Z2 sits directly under the masthead". The masthead is 88px over the 144px folded frame (`SESSION_MINIMIZED_HEIGHT_PX`, `session-card-registration.tsx`; the tier is stated in `fcc60dc88`'s message), which leaves the Z2 row and the beat about 56px. **(verified by reading)**

**[F02] The `sheet-visibility` arc makes a folded card's sheets drop from the masthead over whatever is below.** Its [B04] stands the rest-line anchor down when the slot has no box and its [B05] switches the entrance to `top`, so the AI settings sheet on a folded card is a 586px panel hanging from a 144px frame (measured by `at0558-sheet-visibility`'s `measure-folded` note: content 141..727 against frame 40..184). Its [B03] then lifts the pane so the panel paints over the peer beneath. That arc is audited and unjoined at the time of writing; its [B02] (canvas-sized growth for a tall composer in a short *unfolded* pane) and [B03] (a pane holding a sheet paints above its peers) are right for the cases they were built for and are not what this brief objects to. **(verified; the screenshot the user sent is the reproduction)**

**[F03] The Z2 telemetry placards are not popovers; they are one in-card `TugPlacard`, and it opens upward.** `session-card-telemetry-renderers.tsx:666` — "The Z2 detail surfaces render as ONE card-scoped TugPlacard, toggled open on the activating cell". `tug-placard.tsx`'s header says what that means: it renders **in place** rather than portaling to the canvas overlay, "hides with its card … and never bleeds over a neighbouring pane", at `z-index: 20` inside the chrome's stacking context (`tug-placard.css:36`). The status-row variant places it `bottom: calc(100% + var(--tug-space-sm))` above `.session-card-status-bar` (`session-card-telemetry-renderers.css:129`), i.e. over the transcript's tail. **(verified)** The earlier brief's [F08] read `TugPopover` and was stale.

**[F04] On a folded card the placard has nowhere to go, three times over.** Above Z2 there is no transcript ([F01]), only the masthead and then the frame's top edge, and `.tug-pane-chrome` has `overflow: clip` (`tug-pane.css:201`), so the panel is cut. Its viewport guard sizes the body from the gap between its anchored bottom edge and the **top of the window** (`tug-placard.tsx`, "Viewport guard"), so a folded card near the top of the wall gets a placard a few pixels tall. And `z-index: 20` inside the pane cannot paint over a peer even where the clip does not bite. This is the placard doing exactly what it was designed to do — stay inside the card — on a card that has no inside. **(the mechanism is verified by reading; the height and clip were not measured in DevTools, and one read of the open placard's rect on a folded card would confirm which of the three the user is seeing first)**

**[F05] Inline dialogs live in the transcript and so are folded away entirely.** `TugInlineDialog` is "rendered inline in transcript flow" and is the primitive for `PermissionDialog` and `QuestionDialog` (`tug-inline-dialog.tsx` header). The transcript is the view slot, which is `display: none` and `inert` when folded ([F01]). A question or a permission request arriving on a folded card is therefore invisible and unanswerable until the user unfolds, and nothing on the folded card says one is waiting. The pending state is already held outside the transcript — `side-question-store.ts` for questions, and the permission path's own store — so a folded-card notice can be derived rather than stored. **(verified by reading; not reproduced)**

**[F06] The compaction cover is a sheet raised by a watcher, not by the user.** `session-compaction-run.tsx:223` calls `showSheet` from a store watcher when a compaction run begins; its content is a one-line progress state with a wave (`compaction-progress-sheet.tsx`). It is the one sheet whose whole content would fit in the Z2 row's height. **(verified)**

**[F07] Sheets a user asks for arrive through a menu or a chip on the card itself.** *AI…* is `sessionCommandItem("AI…", "ai", "session.ai")` at `tugapp/Sources/AppDelegate.swift:1193`; *Show Usage*, *Rewind*, *Help* and the rest are the same shape, and the Z2 chips open the same sheets from the card. In every one of these the user has just named the content they want to see. The card already has the gesture for this: a *bidden entry on a folded card opens the fold first* (`session-card.tsx:2858`, the composer's own [B07]), with a helper that dispatches `SET_CARD_FOLDED false` and returns whether it unfolded (`:2738–2746`). **(verified)**

**[F08] The folded frame's height is fixed by the wall tier, not by the card's content.** `fcc60dc88` keys the masthead tier on `data-masthead-kind` rather than on the fold precisely because "a height that followed the card's form would move every card in a packed column on every fold". The folded frame is one size, and the Z2 row is the only band inside it whose content the card controls. **(verified)**

**[F09] The pane lift built by `sheet-visibility` is reusable as-is for any surface that must overhang a folded frame.** `.tug-pane[data-sheet-open]:not([data-sidebar-pane])` lifts a frame to `--tug-z-pane-sheet-open` (8900), written by `raisePaneForSheet` in `tug-sheet.tsx` through a ref-counted `WeakMap`, and the frame itself carries no `overflow`, `transform` or `contain` (`tug-pane.css:1281–1289` forbids adding any). A panel portaled to the frame can therefore hang below it and paint over the peer beneath; only the chrome clips. **(verified)**

## Decisions {#decisions}

**[B01] The folded card has one surface, the Z2 row, and everything the card shows while folded comes out of that row, downward, over the wall.** This is the rule the rest of the brief instantiates. A folded card is a card that has chosen to show one row ([F01]); anything that needs the user while it is folded is said in that row, or hangs from it. Nothing drops from the masthead, nothing grows upward into it, and nothing is painted into the space the fold took away. Downward is where the room is, and it is also where the rest of the card would be if it were open, so a surface hanging from Z2 reads as the card's content showing through rather than as a panel that ignored the fold. What this rules out is the `sheet-visibility` arc's folded branch ([F02]); what it keeps is that arc's unfolded work.

**[B02] Surfaces the user asked for do not present on a folded card at all: the card unfolds and the surface appears where it normally does.** *AI Settings…*, *Show Usage*, *Rewind*, *Help*, a Z2 chip's sheet — in each the user has just named what they want ([F07]). A notice saying "unfold to see this" would ask them to repeat themselves. So a sheet request that arrives while the card is folded, and that was user-initiated, unfolds the card first through the gesture the composer already has (the bidden-entry helper, [F07]) and then raises the sheet, which rises from its rest line exactly as on an open card. No notice, no second click, no folded presentation of the sheet. This is the sharpest cut in the brief and it is what makes the rest small: only *unbidden* arrivals need a folded form.

**[B03] Unbidden arrivals get a fold notice in Z2: one row in the `TugInlineDialog` vocabulary, standing in place of the telemetry cells while something is pending.** A compaction starting, a question from Claude, a permission request — the user did not ask, and may be reading another card ([F05], [F06]). The notice is icon, title, trailing action, in the vocabulary the inline dialogs already use so it reads as the same family. It **replaces** the five telemetry cells for as long as it stands and they return when it clears: the folded frame's height is fixed by the wall tier ([F08]), so the card cannot grow a row, and a card with a question waiting has one thing to say. The notice is derived from the stores that already hold the pending state ([F05], [F06]) and is not a new store.

**[B04] Two tiers of notice, declared by the surface: `inhabit` and `defer`.** A surface small enough to *be* the row inhabits it: the compaction cover becomes "Compacting…" with its wave in Z2 and raises no sheet ([F06]). Everything else defers: the row names what is waiting and offers **Unfold** — "Claude is asking a question · Unfold", "Permission requested · Unfold" — and pressing it unfolds the card, at which point the inline dialog is simply visible in the transcript, or the held sheet is raised. The tier is a declaration on the surface (a `foldPresentation` option on `showSheet` / `TugSheetContent`, defaulting to `defer`) rather than a measurement, because whether a surface fits in one row is a design fact about that surface, not a number to be read off it at runtime. Today the only inhabitant is the compaction cover.

**[B05] Pressing Unfold on the notice unfolds the card and lets the ordinary pointerdown path bring it front.** The notice's button is on the card, so the pane-activation-on-pointerdown path the deck already runs focuses and raises the pane before the click lands; the notice adds nothing to that. What it must do is the same thing the composer's bidden entry does ([F07]): dispatch the unfold, then let the surface present. No new focus rule.

**[B06] A Z2 placard on a folded card opens downward, hangs past the frame, and points up at its chip.** The placard stays a placard on an open card — upward over the transcript's tail is right there ([F03]). Folded, it flips: it anchors to the bottom of the Z2 row, grows down over the wall, and its body is capped against the **visible canvas bottom** rather than the viewport top ([F04]). It keeps its horizontal anchoring under the activating cell, which is the "points to its item" the user asked for. The cells themselves stay live on a folded card, which they already are ([F01]: only the slot and the entry region are `inert`).

**[B07] To overhang, the folded placard leaves the chrome for the pane frame and borrows the sheet lift.** The chrome clips ([F04]); the frame does not, and the lift already exists ([F09]). So in the folded form the placard portals to the pane frame (the `TugPaneFrameContext` a sheet uses), positions itself off the Z2 row's rect in frame coordinates, and the frame wears the same `data-sheet-open` lift while it is up — generalised in name if `sheet-open` reads wrong for a placard, but the same attribute, the same rule and the same ref-count, so a sheet and a placard sharing a frame release it correctly. This keeps the placard card-scoped and moving with its pane, which is the property `TugPlacard` was built for; it does not become a canvas-overlay `TugPopover`. What would revisit this is a case the frame's coordinate space cannot express, and none is known.

**[B08] The folded placard keeps `dismiss="auto"`, and an outside pointerdown that lands on the neighbour it is hanging over closes it.** The placard now sits where clicks on the card beneath land, so the first click on that card both closes the placard and reaches the card. That is the right order: the user aimed at the card, and a placard that swallowed the click to protect itself would make the folded card cost its neighbour a gesture.

**[B09] The `sheet-visibility` arc's unfolded work stands; its folded branch is retired here.** [B02] and [B03] of that brief — canvas-sized growth for a short unfolded pane, and the pane lift — are correct and this brief depends on the lift ([F09]). Its [B04] and [B05] (top-anchor stand-down and the `top` entrance on a boxless rest line) become dead paths under [B01]/[B02] here, because a folded card never raises a sheet into a clip. They are removed in this work along with the `at0558` folded case, and `modal-rest-line.md`'s boxless-slot clause is rewritten to say what now happens instead: a folded card's sheet request is answered by the fold rule, not by the anchor. The arc lands as it is; this brief's arc is cut on top of it.

**[B10] The Session menu item is renamed *AI Settings…*.** `AppDelegate.swift:1193`. One line; it rides with this work because the user named it here and because the notice and the unfold gesture both refer to the surface by that name.

## Open Questions {#open-questions}

- **Does *Choose Session* on an unbound folded card inhabit the row or defer?** An unbound card raises its picker sheet on activation (the `sheet-visibility` test fixture tripped over this). It is unbidden by [B03]'s definition, and the picker is not one row tall, so by [B04] it defers — "No session · Unfold to choose". But an unbound card that the user folded may simply want to stay quiet. The code decides nothing here; the user does. The default if unanswered is defer, which is the conservative reading of [B04].

## Non-goals {#non-goals}

- **A folded presentation of full sheets.** Considered as a compact panel, a scaled sheet, or a sheet that overhangs the folded frame (which is what landed). Rejected by [B01]: a folded card shows one row, and a sheet is not one row. The asked-for case unfolds ([B02]); the unbidden case notices ([B03]).
- **Growing the folded card by a notice row.** Rejected by [F08]: the folded frame is the wall tier's height and a card whose height followed its content would move every card in the column. The notice replaces the cells.
- **Portaling the placard to the canvas overlay.** It would overhang, but it would give up what `TugPlacard` was built for — following its pane with no applier and hiding with a background tab — for a property the pane frame already provides ([F09]). The same reasoning the `sheet-visibility` brief recorded against portaling sheets applies here unchanged.
- **Auto-dismissing the notice.** A notice for a question or a permission stands until the thing it names is resolved or the card is unfolded. It is not a toast.
- **Touching the open card.** The placard's upward form, the sheets' rest line, the inline dialogs in transcript flow — none of it moves. Everything here is keyed on the fold.
- **Changing what folds.** The view slot's `display: none` at rest stays (`session-minimize-reveal-brief.md` [B06]); [F05] works around it rather than restoring it.

## Exit {#exit}

An arc, in one landing, cut on top of `sheet-visibility` once that arc has joined.

The measurement first: open a Z2 placard on a folded card and read its rect and its `--radix-popover-content-available-height` against the frame, which says whether the clip, the guard or the z is what the user sees first ([F04]). Then the shape the work naturally takes: the placard's folded form ([B06], [B07]) in `tug-placard.tsx`, `session-card-telemetry-renderers.tsx` and their CSS, sharing the lift with `tug-sheet.tsx`; the asked-for unfold ([B02]) at the point `showSheet` is entered on a folded card, reusing the composer's bidden-entry helper; the fold notice ([B03], [B04], [B05]) as a Z2 occupant in the status bar, with the compaction cover declaring `inhabit` and the question and permission stores driving the deferred rows; the retirement of the folded sheet branch and the doctrine rewrite ([B09]); and the menu rename ([B10]). The lift's attribute name is decided when the placard first shares it.

Verification is on the running app in the folded form: each Z2 chip's placard whole, hanging below the folded frame, over the peer beneath, pointing at its cell; *AI Settings…* from the menu on a folded card unfolding the card and rising from its line; a compaction starting on a folded card reading "Compacting…" in Z2 with no sheet; a question arriving on a folded card reading its notice, with Unfold revealing the dialog in the transcript. The open card is pinned by what already exists — `at0558`'s unfolded cases, `at0226-usage-sheet`, `at0097-rewind-sheet`, `at0492-compaction-card-modality` — and must not move; `at0558`'s folded case is replaced by cases for the notice and the hanging placard.
