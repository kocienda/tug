# Audit-relative readiness: the fixups the audit found, and the follow-on it needs

**Purpose:** The audit-relative readiness arcs (`eaa48b162`, `88a39a63d`) landed the right architecture: readiness is a derived fact from an audited pair, the engine re-stamps the quiet case, a stale arc re-seats its audit on the Wheel, and the join is a squash and a land. An audit of the landed code on 2026-10-29 found the derivation sound and the gaps around it: one wait with no horizon that strands an arc, a join that does not enforce what the derivation decides, and a cluster of smaller holes sitting exactly where the next steps (the Re-audit button, the triage rung, the three waiting states) will land.

---

## Purpose {#purpose}

The user asked for an honest audit of all the arc/join work before moving on: code quality, coherence, the technical choices, adherence to the tuglaws, and whether the architecture sets up the future. Then: "Do we need to make fixups? Or, can we move on from here with confidence that the codebase is in good shape?"

The answer was: the architecture is right, the core derivation is sound and tested, and the codebase is not yet in a shape to move on from. One fixup arc closes the gaps. This brief carries the audit's findings, the decisions on each, and the follow-on work that the earlier brief (`briefs/audit-relative-readiness-brief.md`) named but that has not landed.

## Evidence {#evidence}

What is good is recorded first, because the fixups below are bounded by it.

**[F01] The derivation is sound and tested.** `join_ready` and `audit_current` in `tugrust/crates/tugarc-core/src/log.rs:887–951` short-circuit `Audited` to "is the pair current" under every wheel reading; a pair-less legacy mark reads stale; no rounds reads not ready; a round is a head move so un-audit needs no special case. The runner composes `audit_declared` as audited *and* current (`tugcast/src/feeds/arc_runner.rs:2218–2224`) so a re-seated audit is never finished by the mark it replaces. Covered by `log.rs:2088–2220`, `show.rs:1425–1530`, `base_motion.rs:1339–1439`, and seven lab scenarios in `tugarclab/src/scenario.rs:664–880`. **(verified)**

**[F02] The engine is one machine with no timers, and the join path carries nothing from the ladder.** `decide_for_arc` (`base_motion.rs:150–205`) is a readable decision table; the wakes are the initial sweep, the GIT_HEAD broadcast, workspace open, turn end, settle, every round line, and bind (`:600–645`). `do_changeset_join` → `join_in_with_progress` → `integrate_join` is merge-tree → land; `join_ledger.rs` and `join_pilot.rs` are gone. All fast verdicts are green: tugarc-core 465 tests, tugcast feed tests 238, eight deck suites 190, `just lint-ts`, `just lint-audit`, `just tugplug-lint`, `just app-test-covers-check`. **(verified, run)**

**[F03] A hung verify latches the arc with no horizon.** The quiet re-stamp's `verify_in` (`tugarc-core/src/surfaces.rs:420–427`) runs each declared check through `sh` with `.status()` and no deadline; the engine sets `entry.in_flight = true` before the spawn (`base_motion.rs:895`) and clears it only after `finish_quiet` returns (`:1014`). An app-test wedge, the shape `CLAUDE.md` documents, leaves the arc reading `audit-stale` on the wire with nothing saying a re-stamp is running; every later wake decides `Skip("in-flight")` (`:158`); a bind does not clear it; `reopen_stale_audit` reopens the record but not the engine's latch. Only a tugcast restart recovers it. [L33] fails on all three counts: no deadline, no named terminal state, no user exit. **(verified)**

**[F04] The join does not enforce what the derivation decides.** `join_in_with_progress` (`tugarc-core/src/ops/join.rs:1777–2100`) and `join_blockers_from_detail` (`:1599`) never read `join_ready`; the press handler (`tugcast/src/feeds/agent_supervisor/control/changeset.rs:1636–1915`) checks only open project, worktree and occupancy; the deck's `evaluateJoinGate` (`tugdeck/src/lib/join-mode-controller.ts:133`) reads nothing about stage, and `enterChanges` (`session-card.tsx:1862–1876`) enters join mode on a stale arc with no check. So on a card bound to an `audit-stale` arc, ⌃⌘C opens the composer under a register that reads "audit is stale — re-auditing" (`arc-join-register.ts:325`), and ⬆ lands the arc unaudited. Only the Arcs rows' Join verb follows `JOINABLE_STAGES` (`arc-verbs.ts:241–280`). `integrate_join` reads `base_head` inside `join_gate` (`join.rs:2394`) so two joins cannot lose-update each other, but it lands on whatever the base is, never on the base the audit stood on. `eaa48b162`'s message and the lab's `hand_joins_a_stale_arc` (`scenario.rs:~854`) call this deliberate; the brief's [B02] says "join refused". **(verified)**

**[F05] A dirty worktree on a base-only move spends a turn and latches.** The `audit_stale_done` branch of `decide_for_arc` (`base_motion.rs:165–183`) returns `Restamp` without consulting `worktree_dirty`, which the ordinary path refuses on (`:190`). `replay_onto` answers `Deferred` (dirt, join journal, `no-rounds`, `tip-moved`), mapped to `QuietReplay::Stopped` (`:1000–1003`), which `quiet_end` turns into `Seat` (`:238–247`) and `finish_quiet` latches as `quiet_failed_tip` (`:1015`). With an idle card that seats a model turn over scratch bytes; with none the latch means the bind seats the audit instead of retrying the quiet path once the tree is clean. **(verified)**

**[F06] An audit that goes stale between the mark and the runner's tick is stopped with a false reason, outside both doors.** `audit_action` (`tugcast/src/feeds/arc.rs:1005–1013`) maps `!audit_declared` to `Stop { Audit, AuditDidNotMark }`. The skill marks, its turn ends, a sibling joins in that window, the arc stops saying the audit did not mark. The stopped record is neither `done` nor live, so `reopen_stale_audit` and `restamp_audit` (`tugarc-core/src/ops/documents.rs:218–263`) both return `Ok(false)`, and the engine gates its stale branch on `done`. It waits for a hand `arc run`. `implement_action` already rotates the audit again in the analogous case (`arc.rs:856–864`). **(verified)**

**[F07] The shade stays down after a silent re-stamp.** The attention key is `<work_id>:offer` (`join_board.rs:188–190`), and `work_id` is deliberately stable across a replay (`:362–368`, test `:635`). `JoinOfferMemory.forget` is called only on the blocked→unblocked edge (`session-card.tsx:3251–3255`). Base move → stale → re-stamp returns the same key, the gate answers `already-shown` (now logged, per the earlier brief's [B05]), and the room stays shut. A fold or full re-audit lands rounds and mints a new key, so only the quiet case is uncovered — and it is the common one. **(verified)**

**[F08] The triage rung mis-sizes after a replay.** `tugplug/skills/arc-audit/SKILL.md:81` runs `git range-diff <audited_at.head>...HEAD`. The three-dot form is `merge-base..A merge-base..B`; after the base moved and the engine replayed, the merge-base is the old fork point and the right range contains the base's commits, which read as unpaired rounds. Reproduced in a scratch repo: one base commit plus one new round after a rebase printed the base commit as `-: > 1:`. The explicit form `git range-diff <audited.base>..<audited.head> <base>..HEAD` pairs correctly in the same probe. The fold rung's `tugtool arc verify --base <audited_at.head>` is a real flag and fine. **(verified, reproduced)**

**[F09] The join press has no horizon on the deck, and the row press swallows a missing store.** `changeset-verb-store.ts` arms no timer on `changeset_join`; `pressArcJoin` (`arc-join-press.ts:61–68`) and `performJoin` (`join-mode-controller.ts:905–920`) release their subscriptions only when the phase leaves `pending`, so an unacknowledged press holds "Joining…" forever and leaks both. The base fold has `PRESS_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_MS` (`changeset-join-store.ts:92`); the join press, now the shade's only act, has nothing. `pressArcJoin` returns `false` when the verb store is null and both callers discard it (`arcs-card.tsx:748`, `session-changes-arc-lane.tsx:471`). [L27], [L31], [L33]. **(verified)**

**[F10] Two readiness authorities remain.** `join_board::join_state_for` (`join_board.rs:114–226`) still runs the `merge-tree` probe (`cached_probe`, `:375`) for every non-blocked arc on every head-pair move and withholds as `"conflicted"` on its answer (`:181–185`). Under [B02] of the earlier brief a ready arc's fit was verified against this exact base, so the probe is a weaker second derivation of a settled fact, paid per base move. **(verified)**

**[F11] The next steps have nothing to read.** The runner computes `audit_current` as a bool; no wire or log fact says *why* the audit went stale (head, base, or both), so the triage rung sizes from git alone. The engine's `in_flight` is private, so a Re-audit press cannot refuse while a quiet job runs. "Waiting for a card" and "re-stamp in progress" are indistinguishable on the wire: both read `stage == "audit-stale" && done`. The waiting states' sentences (`arc-join-register.ts:321–339`) name no card and no door (`tugtool arc run` appears nowhere in the deck). **(verified)**

**[F12] The mark stamps the base read at mark time, not the base the verify ran on.** `mark_in` (`tugarc-core/src/ops/commit.rs:111–127`) rev-parses the live tips when `audited` is written, because a `Current` replay writes no `verified` line. The re-stamp's mark carries no author of record, so a machine re-stamp and a model's audit differ only by note text. **(verified)**

## Decisions {#decisions}

**[B01] The quiet verify gets a horizon, a named state, and a door.** `verify_in` takes a deadline when run by the engine; at the deadline the check is killed and the quiet job ends as `Seat` with a reason the arc's wire detail carries. The engine publishes a per-arc fact for the quiet job (`restamping` with its start time) so the lane and the fold can say "re-stamp running" rather than "stale", and the Re-audit door reads it: a press during a quiet job refuses with that sentence, and a press after the deadline clears the latch. This is [L33]'s three parts, and it is the fix the Re-audit button depends on ([F03], [F11]).

**[B02] The join enforces readiness, and the hand door is explicit.** `join_blockers_from_detail` gains a blocker when `!detail.join_ready` on a Wheel arc, naming why (stale on head, base, or both), delivered through `changeset_join_err` so the deck's existing refusal band and the composer's `landError` render it ([L31] kept). The deck's `evaluateJoinGate` and `enterChanges` read the same fact and refuse to open join mode on a stale arc, with the register's sentence. `tugtool arc join` keeps a hand door as `--unaudited`, which names what it does; the bare verb refuses like the press. The brief's original [B02] ("join refused") stands; the lab's `hand_joins_a_stale_arc` is rewritten to spell the flag. Landing on the exact audited base is then the offer's guarantee plus the gate's, and a compare-and-swap on the pair inside `join_gate` is cheap to add beside the `base_head` read at `join.rs:2394` — do it, since the seat is there ([F04]).

**[B03] A transient deferral leaves; only a conflict or a red seats.** `ReplayOutcome::Deferred` maps to `QuietEnd::Leave` (retry on the next wake), not `Seat`, and the stale-done branch of `decide_for_arc` consults `worktree_dirty` like the ordinary path does. `Seat` is reserved for `Conflicted` and a verify that ran and was red or refused ([F05]).

**[B04] An audit that went stale before the runner ticked rotates the audit, with its own reason.** `audit_action` distinguishes "no mark" from "a mark that is not current": the first stops as `AuditDidNotMark`; the second seats the audit again, as `implement_action` does, and the stop reason vocabulary gains `AuditWentStale` for the case the runner cannot seat (no idle card). A stopped, non-done record whose audit is stale is admitted to `reopen_stale_audit` and the engine's stale branch, so it is inside the doors ([F06]).

**[B05] The stale spell is a reveal edge.** The reveal memory forgets on the attention key's null→non-null edge as it does on blocked→unblocked, so an arc that comes back ready after a re-stamp is new work to show. The key stays `<work_id>:offer`, because the work is the same; what changed is that the user was told it was not ready and now it is ([F07]).

**[B06] The triage rung pairs the two ranges explicitly.** `SKILL.md` §1b runs `git range-diff <audited.base>..<audited.head> <base_branch>..HEAD` and reads the base side with `git diff <audited.base>..<base_branch> --stat`. The readiness detail carries the staleness kind (head / base / both) so the rung declares its size from a fact and not a guess ([F08], [F11]).

**[B07] The join press gets the fold's horizon, and the row press speaks.** `changeset_join` arms the same `PRESS_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_MS` the base fold uses; at the deadline the phase ends in a named state the register and the Join verb render, and both subscriptions release. `pressArcJoin`'s refusal reaches the Arcs row and the lane as the same sentence the composer path already speaks ([F09]).

**[B08] The board reads the derivation and drops the probe.** `join_state_for` withholds on `!detail.join_ready` and on blockers; the `merge-tree` probe, `CachedProbe`, `PROBE_RUNS` and its sweep go, because the audit verified the fit against this base and the one-recompute race is now closed by [B02]'s gate at press time. One authority, in `tugarc-core::log` ([F10]).

**[B09] The engine keys on typed facts, not display words.** `derive_stage`'s string stays the wire's word; the engine's `mid_plan` and `audit_stale_done` and the deck's four `"audit-stale"` literals read one typed predicate exported by `tugarc-core` and one constant in `changeset-types.ts` ([F13]).

**[B10] The mark refuses a base the verify did not see.** `mark_in` cross-checks `last_verified` when it parses: a verify at a different base or head than the live tips refuses the mark with a sentence naming the move, and `fit_fact` is derived from `audited_at` rather than kept as a parallel pair. The re-stamp's mark carries `by: engine` in its note so a reader can tell it from a model's audit ([F12]).

**[B11] Docs and comments say what the code does.** `tracking-changes.md`'s conflicted row reads "re-audits before it joins"; `arc-lifecycle.md`'s stage table lists the nine words with `audit-stale` and names the three waiting states; the "Joining — by reference" section names the Re-audit door. Every comment, test name and docstring in [F13] is rewritten or deleted; `join_resolver.rs` and the `ArcJoinReport*` types are renamed for the crossing they serve (`crossing_resolver.rs`, `CrossingReport*`); `session-question-dialog.css:163` takes the primitive's `min-width`. A reader meeting the pilot's name in a live comment should find none.

**[B12] The follow-on from the earlier brief lands in the same arc, after the fixups.** The Re-audit button in the fold and the Arcs row (earlier [B04]); the three waiting states carrying their card and their door ("re-auditing on `<callsign>`", "waiting for a card — bind one, or `tugtool arc run <name>`"); and the stage-aware triage sentence in the transcript. These depend on [B01], [B02] and [B06] and are ordered after them. A lab scenario covers each: stale-then-ready reveals, a press during a re-stamp refuses, a dirty worktree waits, a mark-to-tick stale rotates, a hung verify ends at its deadline.

## Non-goals {#non-goals}

- **Re-litigating the architecture.** The audited pair, the derived readiness, the engine's quiet case, the Wheel re-seat and the squash-and-land join are right and stay; this brief fixes what stands around them.
- **A gated app-test that drives a real seated re-audit's mark through the deck.** The lab covers the engine and the record; the deck's reveal is unit-tested on the edge; a real-model re-audit under the harness would cost a model turn per run, which the app-test corpus is a tax on.
- **Restoring any rung of the ladder as a fallback.** Rejected by the earlier brief's [B07] and [B08]; nothing here reopens it.
- **A wall-clock deadline on the audit stage's own turn.** The Wheel's turn is a wait on a model, bounded by the runner's existing caps; [B01]'s deadline is on the engine's silent verify only.
