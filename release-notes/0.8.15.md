# Tug 0.8.15

Every step that moves bytes now draws a real progress bar and says what it is doing. You see how much has arrived of how much, how fast, and about how long is left, instead of a bare percentage or a spinner that could have meant anything.

Installing or updating Claude Code is Tug's own download now rather than a script it pipes into a shell, so the row can show you the bar and give you a **Pause** beside it. A pause really keeps what has already arrived: the partial file is on disk, and Resume picks up from the byte it stopped at, even after quitting and coming back. Cancel throws it away.

A Tug update can be stopped mid-download too. That one cannot be resumed — the updater discards what it has when a download ends — so the button says **Stop for Now**, the row tells you how much stopping will throw away before you press it, and the stopped row waits with a Resume rather than reading like something that failed.

While the setup or update wizard is open, the menu bar can no longer change the deck behind it. New Session, New Jot, Open Recent, About, Settings and Keyboard Shortcuts all go dark until it is closed, so nothing opens where you cannot reach it.

## Also in this release

- Clicking the update pill now animates into the wizard with a brief zoom effect, instead of the pill disappearing and the wizard appearing with nothing connecting them.
- A content card's title bar shows a field of dots that lights up on hover, marking where you can grab it to drag the card; a rail (which can't be dragged this way) doesn't show it.
- The Session card's "move to workspace" icon has changed.
- The Changes shade now shows its own refusals (for a blocked commit, discard, or auto-message) inline, right in the shade, instead of in a separate notice elsewhere on the card.
- A reconnecting card now correctly keeps its bound arc visible — arc sigil, step list, and all — instead of momentarily losing track of it.
- Fixed a rare case where an arc's progress could silently stall after a step finished while a background job was still running.
