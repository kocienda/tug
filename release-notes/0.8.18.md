# Tug 0.8.18

## Zoom

- View › Zoom now scales the whole app picture — layout, type, and spacing all grow or shrink together instead of reflowing, and type actually shrinks and grows down to 50%.
- Added ⌥⌘0 to toggle "Actual Size."
- A new Zoom row in the Layout card gives you four quick levels (70/80/90/100%) plus a fifth slot when you're at some other level.
- Zoom steps are noticeably faster and show the target percentage right as you press the key.
- Fixed a flash where folding or unfolding a card while zoomed made part of the card jump or squeeze oddly.

## Other fixes

- The "Restoring sessions…" progress sheet is back for cold app launches, so you see real progress instead of blank cards.
- Command output (Bash) now leads with a spinner or progress ring instead of an info icon.
- ⇧⌘I (Insert File) now works reliably, including inserting images directly as image attachments.
- Transcript receipts (commits, pushes, joins, replies, and more) now share consistent, lined-up padding.
- The compaction progress bar is now narrower and centered.
