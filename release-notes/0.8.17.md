# Tug 0.8.17

## Smoother motion throughout

Cards, sidebars, and panels now settle into place without the small stutters and stray frames that used to show up mid-move — layouts commit to their new size before anything starts sliding, so you won't see a brief flash of bare background or a list re-flowing while it's still in motion.

## Flow strip swipes feel more natural

- A trackpad swipe across the flow strip now ends in one continuous motion: lift your fingers and the cards glide straight to the next stop at the speed you were swiping, instead of coasting, pausing, and then hopping into place.
- A quick flick now carries past the next stop instead of always stopping at the nearest one.
- Touching the trackpad again mid-slide catches the strip right where it is, rather than fighting the animation.

## Smaller interface touches

- The current card's icon no longer gets tinted differently — only the highlighted title bar marks which card you're in.
- Card-arrangement hover bubbles now show the card's name, session details, and what each spot does.
- The Arcs sidebar keeps "Stop" and "Changes" always labeled, while less-used buttons show their icon alone until you hover.
- "Replay onto main" is now just "Replay" (the destination still shows as a tooltip), fixing a row that used to overflow.
- Resizing a card by dragging its edge now keeps it in its slot by default, instead of popping it loose — a new Layout setting lets you switch back to the old behavior.
- After rewinding a conversation, the sidebar now correctly shows the conversation that's left (turn count, last prompt, timing) instead of stale info.

## Long-running commands

Running tests or other long shell commands from a Session card now shows live progress — a line of status and an elapsed-time clock — instead of sitting silent until it finishes.
