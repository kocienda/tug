# Tug 0.8.5

Installing an update works, and stopping your work is now a step you take rather than something the install does on your behalf.

- **Install and Relaunch installs and relaunches.** It had never done either. Tug was holding back Sparkle's relaunch until its own shutdown finished, and Sparkle was waiting for that hold to be released before it asked Tug to shut down — so the press did nothing and the second press said the update was no longer active. The hold is gone; Sparkle asks for the quit itself, and the new version comes up once this one's child processes are.
- **Stopping work in flight is its own step.** The update dialog now has four: check, download, stop work in flight, install and relaunch. The stop-work step names the sessions that are mid-turn and gives you a Stop Work button, and *Install and Relaunch* does not appear until nothing is running. Stop the turns in the cards yourself and the step settles on its own; start a new one and the install button steps back until it is done. The install can never be the thing that ended your work, because it is never offered while there is work to end.
