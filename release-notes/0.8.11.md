# Tug 0.8.11

A release about motion and legibility. Switching workspaces no longer flashes a spray of mis-placed cards halfway through — the old screen is held whole until the new one has assembled underneath it, then dissolved. Moving the reader across a deck now slides every time instead of sometimes landing as a cut, and dragging the sash between two split cards stays smooth on a loaded sidebar.

## Also in this one

- The status strip's cells no longer shift under your eye: each is sized to its widest reading from the first paint, and an arc running its steps reads `Executing`.
- The session chip in the composer has a live dot that breathes and changes colour with the session.
- Drop a file into an editor and it arrives with spaces on the sides that need them — and none on the sides that don't.
- A right-click menu closes when the thing it acts on scrolls out of view, instead of staying put over something else.
- Opening a placard on one folded session card closes the one open on another, so there is only ever one.
- Arc rows in the Arcs card give their top line back to the names, so an arc reads as its whole name rather than trailing off.
- A card arriving into a split column now divides the space evenly instead of letting a sitter keep all of it.
- The update notice is one painted pill, and both it and the Check row tell you which version you are running.
- Mixed monospace and sans text on the same row now sits on one baseline throughout the app.
